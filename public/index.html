How to Play

  1. Pick your standard bet with the chips (100 / 200 / 500 / 1000), or nudge it with + / −.
  2. During the betting window, click any room to stake that the pocong will NOT haunt it. Bet on as many rooms as you like — clicking again stacks more.
  3. Refund any room or Clear all while betting is open.
  4. The pocong haunts exactly one room at random. Bets there are lost.
  5. Every safe room pays out 3× your stake. Come out ahead and the win fanfare plays!
