Awas Ada Pocong

A pocong haunts Mansion Level 1 tonight. It hops down the main hallway and picks one room to enter.

  • Hiding Phase (60 seconds): tap ONE of the 11 rooms and press CONFIRM & HIDE before the clock reaches 00:00. No lock at 00:00 means you are disqualified.
  • The hunt: the pocong walks the Pocong Route door by door. If it enters your room, you are caught. Any other room means you survive and score points.
  • Daring Pick: optionally name a second room. Surviving with it doubles your points — but if the pocong enters either room, you are caught.
  • Streak: each survival raises your multiplier, up to 3×.

Farther rooms down the route are worth more points. Rooms nearer the top are safer to reach but score less.
