👻 How to play

Awas Ada Pocong — hide-and-seek
  1. Press Start hiding. You have 60 seconds to pick one of the 11 rooms and press CONFIRM & PLACE to lock it in.
  2. At 00:00 the Pocong walks the hallway and enters one random room. The further along its route, the more that room is worth.
  3. If it enters your room — FOUND! Any other room — you survived and score points. Surviving in a row raises your streak multiplier.
